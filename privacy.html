Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Transcribe Automation is a personal tool used only by its owner, Steve Tansowny.

What it accesses

With the owner's Google sign-in, it reads the owner's YouTube playlists and moves videos between them. It uses this access only to make transcripts for the owner's personal use.

What it stores

The sign-in token and transcripts are stored only on the owner's own computer and Google Drive. No data is sold, shared or sent to any third party, and no data is collected from anyone else.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
